This circuit uses an Arduino Uno to control multiple LEDs through its digital output pins, where each LED is connected with a resistor to limit current and protect the components. The circuit demonstrates the basic principles of Arduino programming, electronic interfacing, and digital signal control. When the Arduino sends a HIGH signal, the corresponding LED turns on, and when it sends a LOW signal, the LED turns off. This project is commonly used by beginners to learn about microcontrollers, breadboard wiring, and LED sequencing applications.
