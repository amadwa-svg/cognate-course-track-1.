Arduino Uno: Acts as the brain, sending signals to control the 7-segment display and reading input from the push buttons.

7-Segment Display: Each segment is wired to a digital pin through resistors, allowing numbers (0–9) to be displayed.

Push Buttons: Likely used for incrementing or decrementing the displayed number, or toggling between modes.

Resistors: Prevent excessive current from damaging the LEDs in the display or the Arduino pins.
